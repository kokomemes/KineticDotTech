WHAT WE'RE BUILDING

AI workflows, without the fragmentation.

KINETIC is a product concept for a workspace where multiple specialised AI agents can contribute to a single task.

The idea is to make multi-step work understandable: define a goal, see which agent handles each stage, review the result, and keep the human in control.

We're starting with a visual workflow demonstration. Real model integrations, source retrieval, and task execution would require additional development and infrastructure.
